Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

The short version

  • CrispList does not collect or share any personal data, and nothing you add is stored on our servers.
  • Your lists stay on your iPhone. We can't see them.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CrispList: Shopping List ("CrispList," "the app") handles your information. CrispList is developed and published by Adam Drag ("we," "us," or "our"). By using CrispList, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through CrispList. The app does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal details.

Your lists, photos, and app data

The lists, items, and photos you add in CrispList are stored locally on your iPhone. This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate. We have no access to it.

To make adding items faster, CrispList remembers the names of custom items you've added so it can suggest them again, even after they've been removed from a list. These names are stored only on your iPhone.

If you attach a photo to an item, CrispList asks for camera access only when you tap Take Photo. Choosing from your library uses Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick. Photos are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone and are never sent to us.

If you add the CrispList widget to your Home Screen, the app saves a small copy of your first list (its name and up to 12 items to buy) in storage shared only with the widget. This data stays on your device.

Lists and items you delete in the app disappear right away and are erased from your iPhone the first time you open CrispList after 30 days. Deleting CrispList deletes your lists and photos from your iPhone, though a copy may remain in an earlier device backup. We cannot recover deleted data.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your CrispList data, including photos, is included in those backups. iCloud backups are managed by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

CrispList does not contain advertising, does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated information such as crash reports and general usage statistics. This information does not identify you, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Third-party services

CrispList does not share data with third parties. The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download or rate the app, Apple handles that information according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

If you email us for support, we will receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to you, and we do not share it or use it for marketing.

Our website

This Support page and Privacy Policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Our website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking.

Children's privacy

CrispList does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added to the app. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and change the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.
