Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use CrispList?

No. CrispList doesn't use accounts. Just open the app and start making lists.

Where are my lists stored?

Your lists are saved only on your iPhone. They aren't uploaded to us or to any server, and we can't see them. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does CrispList work without internet?

Yes. Everything in CrispList works offline, so you can use your list anywhere, including stores with poor reception.

How do I back up my lists?

Open Settings in CrispList and use Export to create a backup file. You can save it to the Files app, iCloud Drive, or send it to another device with AirDrop.

To bring your lists back, open Settings in CrispList, choose Restore, and select your backup file.

How do I move my lists to a new iPhone?

The most reliable way is to export a backup on your old iPhone, then restore it on the new one:

  1. On your old iPhone, open CrispList Settings and tap Export.
  2. Save the file to iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it to your new iPhone.
  3. On your new iPhone, install CrispList, open Settings, tap Restore, and choose the file.

If you set up your new iPhone from a full device backup, your lists will usually come along too, but exporting first is the surest way.

What happens to my lists if I delete the app?

Deleting CrispList also deletes your lists from your iPhone. Because nothing is stored on our servers, we can't recover them. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

How do I add the CrispList widget to my Home Screen?
  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top-left corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for CrispList, choose a size, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Tap Done.
Can I sync or share lists with other people?

Not in the current version. Lists stay on the iPhone where you create them. You can still move them to another device using Export and Restore.

Is CrispList free?

Yes. CrispList is free to download from the App Store, with no ads and no account required.

Is CrispList available on Android?

No. CrispList is made for iPhone, and there is no Android version.
